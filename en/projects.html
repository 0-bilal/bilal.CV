QB-Sentinel

Operations, restaurants & inventory

Goodbye to daily operations paperwork

In juice shops, paper piles up every day: attendance sheets, inspection and cleaning logs, end-of-day reports. Information gets lost and follow-up is delayed. QB-Sentinel was born from real work at Juice Time to bring all of that into one mobile app, so the manager knows what is happening in the branch as it happens, never misses a residency or contract renewal, and quality standards stay documented every shift.

Features

  • Staff attendance, with reminders to renew residency permits and contracts.
  • Fruit inspection logs, ingredient standards and equipment cleaning.
  • Product availability, return requests and raw-material production.
  • End-of-day report and cash payment voucher.

QB-Prax

Operations, restaurants & inventory

Kitchen orders on screen, no pagers

Printed tickets and pagers slow the kitchen down at peak times and mix up the order of orders. QB-Prax turns a restaurant running Foodics into a digital kitchen without changing the POS: it captures each receipt the moment it prints and shows the order on the kitchen screen automatically, so the team knows what to prepare first, with fewer mistakes and shorter waits for customers.

Features

  • Captures print data from an Epson printer over the network.
  • Rebuilds the receipt image and reads the order number with OCR.
  • Shows orders automatically on the kitchen screen instead of pagers.

QB-Nexa
