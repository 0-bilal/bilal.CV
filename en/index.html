About me

A hands-on person who likes to understand work from the inside: I do it myself, notice where it gets stuck, then look for a better way to get it done. I believe organization is the foundation of any successful business, and that every problem holds an opportunity to improve.

I started working at a young age in 2015 and moved between restaurants, shops and other fields, learning something new from each experience: from customer service and food preparation to team leadership, purchasing and training. I turn many of the solutions I reach into software under the name QB software, saving time, reducing errors and making work easier for the people who use it.

I keep learning, and I enjoy turning chaos into order.

QB-Sentinel

Goodbye to daily operations paperwork

In juice shops, paper piles up every day: attendance sheets, inspection and cleaning logs, end-of-day reports. Information gets lost and follow-up is delayed. QB-Sentinel was born from real work at Juice Time to bring all of that into one mobile app, so the manager knows what is happening in the branch as it happens, never misses a residency or contract renewal, and quality standards stay documented every shift.

PWAOpen source

View the project on GitHub

QB-Prax

Kitchen orders on screen, no pagers

Printed tickets and pagers slow the kitchen down at peak times and mix up the order of orders. QB-Prax turns a restaurant running Foodics into a digital kitchen without changing the POS: it captures each receipt the moment it prints and shows the order on the kitchen screen automatically, so the team knows what to prepare first, with fewer mistakes and shorter waits for customers.

PythonSQLiteOCR

QB-Ledg

Every riyal of a cash advance on record

Cash advances are one of the biggest causes of lost money and disputes at settlement. I lived this problem myself in purchasing, so I built QB-Ledg to solve it. It records every advance and what was spent from it, and produces print-ready reports, making settlement easy and clear for both sides.

QB-FLOW

Your restaurant or company on one platform

Restaurant and company work is spread across many programs that don't talk to each other, so data gets lost between them and the team wastes time switching. QB-FLOW brings management, the call center, customers, the menu and reception into one platform, giving management the full picture and helping the team serve customers faster.

PHPSQLDockerNginx

QB-Dine

Beyond a menu, beyond the ordinary

A modern digital platform for restaurants that changes how products are presented, how customers are served and how daily work is organized. From an interactive digital menu to the cashier screen and admin dashboard, QB-Dine brings a restaurant's essential tools into one connected experience. Because a menu should do more than list products: QB-Dine gives guests an engaging digital experience and simplifies the team's daily work with modern, accessible web technology. One platform, a better experience, smarter operations.

PWAQR

QB-Eligibility Pro

Know which bank will approve your client before you apply

Every bank has its own criteria, and submitting a client to a bank that won't approve them wastes days and weakens the client's trust. QB-Eligibility Pro compares the client's data with each bank's criteria and shows the result instantly, so you send the client to the right bank the first time.

LocalStorage

QB-BluePulse

An always-clean pool, no forgotten tasks

Pool maintenance depends on consistency, and a forgotten task or a wrong chemical dose means unsafe water. QB-BluePulse sets a clear monthly maintenance schedule, tracks what has been done and calculates chemical quantities precisely, all from your phone.

QB-Nexa

Know a deal's profit before you offer it

Working out a car-finance deal by hand takes many numbers: the bank price, VAT, plates, cashback and support, and one small mistake can cost the profit or the customer. QB-Nexa calculates it all in seconds, shows the net profit clearly and prepares the offer message ready to send, so the salesperson closes deals faster and with confidence. It has been used in a real car showroom.
